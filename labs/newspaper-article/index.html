Mars Colony Establishes First Library

A new era for interstellar education and literature begins today

By Commander Sarah Chen

The red planet is no longer just a site for mining and scientific research. With the opening of the Olympus Heights Library, residents now have access to over ten thousand physical volumes and a vast digital archive of Earth's history.

Citizens gathered at the airlock entrance to witness the ribbon-cutting ceremony. This milestone marks a significant shift in colonial culture, prioritizing the arts alongside survival in the harsh Martian environment.
